# Skyrim SE decompilation milestones

**Where we are (24 November 2026 UTC).** 38,805 functions marked `matched` in [`data/progress.json` on `origin/main`](https://github.com/Mudcrab-Team/skyrim_decomp/blob/b13164e1127de04df263e909d15ee981693b7d7c/data/progress.json), updated at 21:15 UTC. Overall scope is still unknown.

- **11–14 November.** Pinned Steam Skyrim SE 1.7.104, accepted the first functions, published the progress site and opened the contributor workflow.
- **15–22 November.** Added hash verification in Windows CI, moved acceptance to a single PR, automated intake and admitted additional sources for names and drafts.
- **23–24 November.** Folded drafting lessons into the cookbook, indexed the x64 siblings, published target hashes for supported ordinary functions and tested larger generated and model-assisted batches.

Dates below are in November 2026 UTC. The **When** column links to landed commits or PRs. Open PRs are dated by submission. A local comparator pass, a shape lead and a match recorded in the merged inventory are different results.
